SEO content requests for this site cover every public content page, including individual journal articles—not only the homepage or selected canvas preview.

**Why:** The user repeated that optimization must include every page after earlier changes focused too narrowly.

**How to apply:** Inventory live marketing, legal, journal-index and article URLs first, then verify coverage per URL. Legal pages need accurate explanations rather than commercial keyword padding. Preserve news sources, stable URLs, service boundaries and real client permissions. Broader DataForSEO keyword estimates are topic evidence, not measured volume for every Bali-specific long tail or a guarantee of visitors.

Every canonical public page needs its own subject-relevant primary picture and a matching sharing image, following the site's Bali accounting concept. Shared logos and article teaser cards are legitimate; generic primary-cover reuse is not.

**Why:** The user emphasised that unique pictures on every page, close relevance and visual alignment are very important.

**How to apply:** Include published-only Journal articles in the inventory; the development and production article lists may differ. Check actual photo identities, not filenames or cache-busting URLs. Preserve approved later image replacements. Keep canonical routes, image inventory and sitemap coverage consistent, and validate internal links and image availability before publishing.
