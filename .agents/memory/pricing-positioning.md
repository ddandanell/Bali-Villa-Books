name: Pricing positioning
description: The owner's market-positioning requirement for Bali Accounting prices.
---

The owner wants Bali Accounting pricing to be market-adjusted: “Not too cheap, but it needs to make sense.”

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a credible, reasonably priced offer rather than simply the lowest price.

**How to apply:** Check comparable published bookkeeping offers when revisiting prices. Distinguish bookkeeping from broader tax, legal and villa-management services; do not infer a market average or profitability from a small sample.
