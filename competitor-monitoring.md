# Bali accounting and business-support service comparison

Last reviewed: 9 October 2026. This is an internal editorial brief, not public testimonials or a performance audit.

These are descriptions of the providers' own public copy. Their credentials, customer totals, reviews, approval rates, traffic, rankings and financial performance were not independently verified. No claim that Bali Accounting outperforms them is made.

## Implemented content position

- Keep practical villa bookkeeping and owner reporting as the existing accounting foundation.
- Present the user-confirmed in-house company, tax and visa services as separate engagements.
- Give each new service an audience, document checklist, process, deliverables, worked scenario, limits, FAQs and quotation basis.
- Distinguish company registration, business identification/licensing, tax administration, immigration permissions and management reports.
- Preserve existing monthly prices. Do not imply add-on services are included.
- Use service-specific WhatsApp enquiries; the existing saved enquiry form remains villa-bookkeeping focused.
- No fabricated testimonials, professional licences, government affiliation, guaranteed tax savings or approval promises.

## Official reference portals checked

- OSS: https://oss.go.id/ — business identification, risk-based licensing and reporting resources.
- Directorate General of Taxes: https://www.pajak.go.id/en — tax information and registration/reporting service links.
- Indonesian e-Visa: https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/ — application selection and status resources.

These checks establish available reference portals, not individual eligibility or a complete legal review. The attempted older e-Visa FAQ URL returned 404 and is deliberately not used.
