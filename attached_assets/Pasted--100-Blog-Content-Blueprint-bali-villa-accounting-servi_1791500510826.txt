3. **Worked numbers** — every tax post needs at least one IDR/USD example calculation.

5. **"What owners get wrong" section** — 3 myths, corrected.

7. **FAQ block (4–6 questions)** — mark up with FAQ schema.

## 8. Quick-Reference Fact Base (verified Oct 2026 — re-verify before each publish)

- Individual resident rental income: **10% final tax (PPh 4(2)) on gross rent**; non-resident: **20% withholding (PPh 26)**.
- PT PMA: **22% corporate tax on net profit**; dividends to foreign shareholders **20% WHT**, reducible to 10–15% by treaty with valid Form DGT/Certificate of Domicile.
- **PHR (PBJT): 10% regional accommodation tax**, collected from guest, remitted monthly to regency (Bapenda); needs NPWPD. **OTAs do not withhold it.**
- **VAT 11%** only above IDR 4.8B annual turnover (PKP). PHR and VAT don't stack on the same service.
- **BPHTB 5%** acquisition duty; **PBB ~0.1–0.5% of NJOP** annually; **2.5% final tax on sale**; PT PMA share transfer can carry **5% on gross**.
- Monthly filing rhythm: payment by ~15th, filing by ~20th of following month; annual corporate return **April 30**; fines IDR 100K–1M per late return + interest.
- **NIB verification via OSS** required for OTA listings (Airbnb/Booking.com) — enforcement deadline was March 31, 2026; delistings ongoing.
- Foreigners can't hold Pondok Wisata personally — PT PMA + **KBLI 55193** is the compliant villa route; >5 rooms needs Melati/TDUP.
- Bali Tourist Levy **IDR 150,000 per foreign guest**; STM/APOA guest reporting within 24 hours.
- Home countries: AU (ATO worldwide income, FITO credit), UK (arising basis since April 2025), US (FBAR/FATCA), SG (territorial). DTAs **do not** reduce Indonesian tax on rental income (Article 6) — they mainly reduce **dividend** withholding.
