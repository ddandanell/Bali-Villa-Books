## Why

QA's post-merge read of #17/#18 (`needs_review`, checklist items 2 and 8) found that the credit correction in #18 never reached the page a reader sees. #18 rewrote the HTML document. `app.js` removes `<main id="journal-server-content">` on mount and `BlogArticle` renders `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` from `/api/blog/posts/<slug>`, which #18 did not touch. So 31 pages still showed the old credit under the new art, and four of them named an identified third party (Erfgoedcel Denderland ×2, City of Chichester, Coronelli) under an AI-generated file.

QA also found the matcher only accepted an empty `Photo credit: .` or `Image credit:`, so named credits survived, and that `accounting-services-bali-monthly-deliverables` had been given a second caption because the caption pass was keyed on text rather than on whether the art changed.

## What changed

`api/gateway.mjs`

1. **JSON pass.** `/api/blog/posts` and `/api/blog/posts/:slug` now get the same treatment as the document: hero/share image, alt text, the credit paragraph inside `contentHtml`, and the related-post card images. Keyed on the old value, so it is a no-op once the backend serves the same art.
2. **The client seed is parsed, not regexed.** `window.__BLOG_INITIAL__` is located by brace-matching, parsed, transformed with the same `repointPost`, and re-serialised with `<`, `>` and `&` escaped. Patching escaped fragments was missing whole classes of credit and could not be kept in step with the API.
3. **Credits are keyed on the start of the paragraph** (`Illustrative photo`, `Image credit:`, `Photo credit:`, `AI-generated editorial illustration`). Named third-party credits are now caught; body copy that merely mentions a credit note, credit card or credit check is not touched.
4. **Exactly one credit paragraph survives**, holding the caption recorded in `journal-imagery.json`. No paragraph is inserted where a page had none. Scope and qualification paragraphs are vetoed from ever being treated as a credit.

`social-images.ts` / `site-content`

5. `updateShareImage` now asks whether this build ships art for the slug (`localArticleArt`) instead of calling `articleVisual`, which always returns an object. The 8 articles with no local art were declaring `og:image:width/height` 1200×630 for a 1280×2104 Wikimedia scan.

`test-journal-art.mjs` — named credits, the two-paragraph collapse, the scope veto, the API record, both JSON endpoints, the seed round-trip, and second-pass stability.

## Scope decision beyond QA's minimum

The credit rewrite also touches articles whose art was already local and whose credit read "publisher-selected open-license image library".
