## Why

QA's post-merge read of PR #20 found the residual of the same class as its items 2 and 8. `repointPost` in `api/gateway.mjs` returned early when the **parent** record had no entry in `journal-imagery.json`:

```js
const art = journalArt[post.slug];
if (!art) return post;                 // early return
...
if (Array.isArray(out.relatedPosts)) out = { ...out, relatedPosts: out.relatedPosts.map(repointPost) };
```

So the `relatedPosts` of the 8 no-art records were never visited. Eight cards, one on each of the 8 no-art pages, still served the Wikimedia scan for `direct-booking-payment-processor-reconciliation-villa` with alt "Illustrative photo: Dr. A. Louise Klehm's Day Book and Ledger … (page 119)", while that article's own page had shown `direct-booking-…-2026-11.webp` and the AI caption since #20. The card contradicted the page it linked to.

Measured before the fix, apex, `/api/blog/posts/villa-cash-flow-forecast-seasonal-income`: parent image Wikimedia (correct, that article has no local art), card `direct-booking-…` image Wikimedia page 119, alt naming the ledger.

## What changed

`api/gateway.mjs` — the `relatedPosts` mapping is lifted above the early return. A record with no local art keeps its own `imageUrl`, `imageAlt` and credit paragraph exactly as the origin serves them; only its cards are transformed.

`scripts/src/test-journal-art.mjs` — five assertions added: a no-art parent keeps its own image and credit, its card pointing at an art article takes that article's local art, the card alt matches the target page, the rewrite is stable on a second pass, and the input record is not mutated.

The test was proved able to fail: run against the pre-fix `repointPost` it throws
`AssertionError [ERR_ASSERTION]: a related card pointing at an art article must show its local art`, exit 1. Restored, it passes.

## Corrections to release 2026-10-31f

QA's read of #20 found five statements in `2026-10-31f.md` that were wrong or overstated. f.md now carries a corrections block; the substance is:

1. "Exactly one credit paragraph survives" → **at most one**. Measured on the API record for all 68 art pages: 44 carry the recorded caption, 24 carry none. The code deliberately never inserts a credit paragraph where a page had none.
2. "Records with local art | 68 — image path, alt and credit paragraph all correct" → the image path and alt are correct on all 68; the credit paragraph is present and correct on 44.
3. "Records without local art | 8 — untouched, Wikimedia credit retained because it is correct" → their own image and credit are untouched by the card fix, but their **related cards** were not, which is what this release corrects. And the credit is not correct on 4 of the 8 (see below).
4. "Related-post cards repointed | 67" → #20 corrected 67 cards; 8 more were missed by the early return and are corrected here. The live corpus holds 228 cards, 225 local, 3 Wikimedia.
5. "Credit paragraphs per article | ≤ 1 everywhere" → true of art pages; one no-art page carries two.

## Open, not fixed here

These live on the 8 no-art pages and cannot be reached through `journalArt`, because there is no local art recorded for those slugs. They are pre-existing origin data, not regressions from #20 or #24.

- **3 pages carry an empty credit**: `/blog/villa-cash-flow-forecast-seasonal-income`, `/blog/villa-petty-cash-reconciliation-vouchers-floats-counts` and `/blog/villa-bookkeeping-duplicate-invoices-credit-notes` all render "Illustrative photo, not a client engagement. Photo credit: . Public domain."
- **1 page carries two credit paragraphs**: `/blog/villa-management-accounting-conflict` has "Image credit: publisher-selected open-license image from the Bali Accounting image library…" followed by "Illustrative photo… Photo credit: See original attribution. Public domain." Under a Wikimedia scan that names no author, both are provenance claims with no source. Correcting either means a fifth edge transform or the prepared journal-repair SQL on the origin, which is a decision rather than a patch.
- **24 art pages show an AI-generated hero with no disclosure.** Not a wrong credit, but a transparency gap. A caption could be inserted on those 24 without touching anything else; that is a product decision, not a defect fix.

## Rollback

`git revert c4bffee3` restores the early return. Production would then show the stale ledger scan on 8 related cards while the articles they link to show local art — worse than the current state, so the revert is only useful if this release is suspected of something else.
