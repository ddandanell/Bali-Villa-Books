# Bali Accounting pricing review

Checked 5 October 2026. This is positioning based on a small sample of public asking prices, not a market median, a profitability study or evidence of completed transactions.

## Verified comparable offers

- [VillaTax monthly bookkeeping](https://villa-tax.operium.store/services/accounting/monthly-bookkeeping): advertised Rp 2,900,000/month. Bookkeeping and reconciliation are comparable; tax filing, payroll and legal work are excluded from that service.
- [Palma accounting services](https://www.palmaislands.com/en/more/accounting-services): advertised basic Rp 2,500,000 and full Rp 5,500,000/month. Its advertised tax/annual services, and full-package payroll/LKPM work, make it a broader offer rather than an identical comparison.
- [Malina accounting](https://www.malinavisa.com/en/accounting): quotation-based. No numerical benchmark inferred.
- Villa-management percentage commissions are not bookkeeping comparables.

## Revised public offer

| Service | Public price | Positioning |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Starter | Rp 2,900,000/month | Comparable bookkeeping anchor; maintains the stated one-villa, two-channel scope. |
| Professional | Rp 4,900,000/month | More owner reporting, channel and staff-record visibility; below the cited broader Rp 5.5m full accounting offer, without claiming identical tax or filing services. |
| Premium | Rp 6,900,000/month | Increment for forecast/variance analysis, annual position summary and prioritized review; no tax advice or filings. |
| Historical cleanup | From Rp 7,900,000 | Reviewed project starting fee, up to 12 months; not an unlimited fixed-price reconstruction. |

Cleanup has no verified like-for-like fixed public comparator in this research. Its amount is a service-positioning decision, not a claim about the market average. Volume, document quality, opening balances and exceptions must be scoped before quoting; a fee cannot be proven profitable without the firm's actual costs.

Property/entity/transaction/staff limits beyond the stated Starter scope remain subject to written agreement. Software, onboarding, taxes on fees and any third-party charges must be confirmed; do not invent inclusions. Monthly delivery by the 15th depends on complete and timely documents.

The report's automated steps describe an agreed workflow requiring selected tools, configured integrations and human approvals. The website is not a live client-accounting platform. Forecasts and demonstrations do not guarantee savings, returns or outcomes.
